Autor: Alem Sakoč (Coksa), hello@alemcoksa.com.

Lokacija

Ako dozvoliš, Hava pita telefon za tvoju lokaciju da pokaže prognozu za mjesto gdje jesi. Lokacija se ne šalje autoru i ne pamti se nigdje osim na tvom telefonu. Da bi se dobila prognoza, koordinate mjesta šalju se servisima ispod, kao i kod svake aplikacije za vrijeme.

Šta se šalje i kome

  • Open-Meteo (prognoza, kvalitet zraka, pretraga mjesta): koordinate mjesta ili ono što upišeš u pretragu.
  • RainViewer (radar) i OpenFreeMap (mapa): koji dio mape treba.
  • OSRM (Putujem): početak i cilj puta.
  • DJI (zone leta, samo Android): područje oko mjesta.
  • MeteoAlarm i aviationweather.gov (upozorenja, mjerenja s aerodroma): ništa o tebi, samo opšti podaci.
  • GitHub (provjera nove verzije): samo pitanje ima li nova verzija.

Šta ostaje na telefonu

Sačuvana mjesta, izbor drona i aktivnosti, zadnja prognoza (da radi i bez interneta) i dnevnik grešaka. Dnevnik se nikome ne šalje sam: kad ima grešaka, vidiš link ispod verzije aplikacije i možeš ga poslati mailom ako želiš prijaviti grešku.

Obavijesti

Ako ih uključiš, Hava u pozadini otprilike svakih 15 minuta provjeri prognozu za tvoje mjesto, direktno s telefona.

Pitanja preko asistenata

Pitanja preko Ostrva i drugih asistenata idu na Havin server (Vercel) samo kao tekst pitanja i ime mjesta. Server ništa ne pamti o tome ko pita.

Djeca

Hava nije namijenjena posebno djeci i ne prikuplja lične podatke.

Brisanje

Deinstaliraj aplikaciju ili obriši njene podatke u postavkama telefona i ništa ne ostaje.

Privacy policy: Hava

Last updated: 28 November 2026.

Hava has no user accounts, ads or analytics, and does not sell or share your data.

Developer: Alem Sakoč (Coksa), hello@alemcoksa.com.

Location

If you allow it, Hava asks your phone for your location to show the forecast where you are. Your location is never sent to the developer and is stored only on your phone. To get a forecast, the coordinates are sent to the services below, as with any weather app.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Open-Meteo (forecast, air quality, place search): coordinates or your search text.
  • RainViewer (radar) and OpenFreeMap (map): which part of the map is needed.
  • OSRM (Travelling): start and destination of the route.
  • DJI (flight zones, Android only): the area around the place.
  • MeteoAlarm and aviationweather.gov (warnings, airport observations): nothing about you.
  • GitHub (update check): only whether there is a new version.

Stored on your phone

Saved places, drone and activity choices, the last forecast (for offline use) and an error log. The log is never sent automatically; you can email it yourself to report a bug.

Notifications

If enabled, Hava checks the forecast for your place about every 15 minutes in the background, directly from your phone.

Questions via assistants

Questions asked through Ostrvo or other assistants reach Hava's server (Vercel) only as the question text and place name. The server keeps nothing about who asked.

Children

Hava is not directed at children and collects no personal data.

Deletion

Uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone settings, and nothing remains.
